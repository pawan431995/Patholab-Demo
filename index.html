Everything the laboratory needs

The same screens and rules as the PathoLab desktop application.

Patients and orders

Register patients once and reuse their history. Create orders with investigations or health packages, price lists, discounts and part payments.

Sample tracking

Follow every sample from collection to storage or disposal. Labels, transfers, rejections and recollections are all logged.

Result entry and checks

Numeric, qualitative, semi-quantitative and narrative results. Age and gender reference ranges, automatic high and low flags and critical-value alerts.

Delta check

Each result is compared with the patient's last verified value. Large changes need a comment before they can be verified.

Reports and signatures

Provisional and final reports with a signature code tied to the verified results. Any edit made after signing is detected.

Quality and operations

Quality control with z-score rules, inventory with lots and expiry, equipment logs, outsourced tests and analyser import (CSV, ASTM, HL7).

Patient messaging

Send report-ready and payment reminder messages. Report PDFs are sent only to patients who have agreed, and each send asks for confirmation.

Billing, MIS and audit

Receipts, invoices, doctor commission, expenses and profit and loss. Management reports on volume, turnaround and compliance, plus a tamper-evident audit log.

How a test moves through the lab

Each step is recorded, and only the allowed transitions can be used.

  1. Register the orderThe receptionist selects tests or a package and records payment.
  2. Collect the sampleThe sample is labelled, collected and received in the lab.
  3. Enter the resultsThe technician enters values. Flags and alerts are applied automatically.
  4. VerifyThe pathologist checks the results, including any delta-check comments.
  5. Issue the reportA signed report is printed, saved as PDF or shared with the patient.
  6. Settle the billDues, receipts and doctor payouts are tracked in Billing and Finance.

Common questions

Is patient data sent to a server?

Not in this web demo. Everything is stored in your browser only. The desktop application keeps data on the laboratory's own computer or database server.

Can I use the demo for real patients?

No. The demo data is fictional and the web demo is not for clinical use.

Does it need an internet connection?

The web demo works offline once the page is open. The desktop application works on the lab's network.

How do I start with my own lab?

Contact us and we will set up your branches, test catalogue, price lists and users, then train your staff.
